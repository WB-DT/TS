# Metrics Guide

All elapsed times use UTC epoch seconds. The application stores each status transition in `quote_status_history` in the same transaction that updates `quotes.status`; do not update a quote's status directly in a reporting or import process. A change to a previously recorded status path must also insert its history row so cycle-time metrics remain complete.

## Quote Cycle Times

**Source:** `v_quote_cycle_times`  
**Grain:** One row per quote.  
**Unit:** Seconds; divide by 86,400 for days.

The view orders a quote's history rows by `changed_at` and uses the next history timestamp as the end of each state interval. For the latest status, it uses `quotes.updated_at`. It then sums state intervals into these measures:

- `screening_seconds`: time in Submitted, Qualifying, and Needs info.
- `waiting_to_assign_seconds`: time in Ready to assign.
- `quoting_seconds`: time in Assigned, In progress, and Returned to quoter.
- `final_review_seconds`: time in In revision and Returned to reviser.
- `approval_seconds`: time in Awaiting approval.
- `total_seconds`: `quotes.updated_at - quotes.created_at`, covering the full recorded quote lifetime to its current status.

Approved, Sent to customer, and Declined are terminal and are not assigned to a stage-duration column. They are still included in `total_seconds`. For a quote with no history, the stage values are zero; seeded and application-created quotes always receive an initial history row.

For average stage time, average the view's corresponding `*_seconds` column over the intended quote population. State whether zero-duration or currently open quotes are included. The view returns per-quote durations and does not bake in a date/status filter.

## Quoter Workload

**Source:** `v_quoter_workload`  
**Grain:** One row per active Quoter.  
**Measure:** `open_quotes`.

Count quotes where `assigned_quoter_id` matches the Quoter and status is not Approved, Sent to customer, or Declined. Submitted, Qualifying, Needs info, Ready to assign, Assigned, In progress, In revision, Awaiting approval, Returned to quoter, and Returned to reviser count as open when assigned. Active Quoters with no open work remain in the view with a count of zero.

For a workload snapshot at a historical date, this current-state view is insufficient on its own; reconstruct assignments/status from timestamped history or add assignment-history events before making historical workload claims.

## Reviser Outcomes

**Source:** `v_reviser_outcomes`  
**Grain:** One row per active Reviser.  
**Population:** Quotes with that user as `final_reviewer_id` and current status Approved, Sent to customer, Declined, Returned to quoter, or Returned to reviser.

- `decisions`: count of included quote outcomes.
- `approved_count`: quotes in Approved or Sent to customer.
- `denied_count`: quotes in Declined.
- `changes_requested_count`: quotes in Returned to quoter or Returned to reviser.
- `approved_rate_percent`: `approved_count / decisions * 100`.
- `denied_rate_percent`: `denied_count / decisions * 100`.
- `changes_requested_rate_percent`: `changes_requested_count / decisions * 100`.

Rates are zero when the denominator is zero. The three rates add to 100% when the decision count is nonzero. A later status transition can change a quote's outcome and therefore its current rate; use `quote_status_history` for as-of reporting. A Reviser who only screens a quote is not credited with its final outcome.

## OCR Field Edit Rate

**Source:** `v_ocr_accuracy`  
**Grain:** One row per template and field name.

- `extracted_field_count`: distinct nonempty OCR baselines in `quote_extracted_fields.original_value` for the template/field.
- `edited_field_count`: distinct extracted-field records with at least one related `field_edits` row.
- `edited_percent`: `edited_field_count / extracted_field_count * 100`.
- `unchanged_percent`: `100 - edited_percent`.

The view excludes null and empty original OCR values. Multiple edits to one extraction count as one edited field. An edit is a correction signal, not proof the final value is correct; `unchanged_percent` is an unreviewed/unchanged proxy and must not be presented as independently verified OCR accuracy. Segment by field and template before comparing workloads because extraction difficulty varies by field and document type.

`field_edits.original_value` stores the first OCR value; `new_value` stores each Quoter-submitted replacement. `quote_extracted_fields.current_value` stores the latest saved value. Manual line items that were not generated by OCR are not included in the OCR extraction denominator.

## Pricing Logic

Plain-English rules for the pricing engine in `pricing.js`, which replicates the company's Cross Reference Tool.

**Matching.** Line items are matched to report rows on item number and UOM together. The Costing report uses `Target Item`; the price reports use `Item Number`.

**Financial cost.** The cost for a line is the lowest value above zero of four sources, in this priority of comparison: (a) the Costing report's `Direct Cost` at this UOM; (b) the Account price report's `Current Cost` at this UOM; (c) a converted cost — take the first Costing row for the item with `Direct Cost` above zero as the base UOM, compute the ratio of the account `Current Cost` at this UOM to the account `Current Cost` at the base UOM, and multiply the base `Direct Cost` by that ratio; (d) the House account price report's `Current Cost` at this UOM. The result is rounded up to two decimals. Items whose prefix appears in the cost-exempt settings list (default CSI, JSW, NWL) have a blank cost. The winning source is stored in `line_pricing.cost_source`.

**Margins and target price.** Current margin is `(account price − cost) ÷ account price`. The target margin is editable per line and defaults from settings. Price at target is `ROUNDUP(cost ÷ (1 − target margin), 2)`.

**Deviation needed.** `Yes` when the winning cost is below the account cost and equals the house cost; otherwise `No`.

**Export calculations.** For the "Extended Prices – Qty & GP Margin" template: extended competitor = competitor price × qty; our price matched to the competitor UOM = (our sell price ÷ our count per UOM) × competitor count per UOM; extended ours = matched × qty; savings % = (competitor price − matched) ÷ competitor price; savings $ = extended competitor − extended ours; extended cost = (extended ours ÷ our sell price) × cost; margin = (sell − cost) ÷ sell. Blank is shown instead of an error when a value cannot be computed.

## Template Notes

Export templates are defined in `templates.js`. Each column declares its key, header text (copied exactly from the source Excel file), Excel column letter, source (`ocr`, `priceReport`, `crossReference`, `quoter`, or `calculated`), formula, and `internalOnly`. Columns marked `internalOnly` (Cost, Extended Cost, Margin, Target Margin, Price at Target Margin, Pricing Method) appear only in the Internal Excel export and are removed from the Customer Excel export. Both exports place the account name and number in the top rows, keep the template's headers and number formats, write live formulas for calculated columns, and include only filled rows. New templates can be added in the same format.

## Role Home Metrics

Each role's home page opens with a "Today" section (due today, waiting on me, overdue, sorted ASAP first) followed by clickable metric cards. Formulas and sources:

**Rep.** My open requests = my quotes not in Approved/Sent to customer/Declined. Ready to send to customer = my quotes in Approved. Needs my fixes = my quotes in Needs info. Average turnaround = mean `total_seconds` from `v_quote_cycle_times` over my quotes.

**Qualifier.** Waiting to screen = quotes in Submitted or Qualifying. Oldest waiting = max age of those. Screened today = transitions to Ready to assign/Declined by me today (from `quote_status_history`). Decline rate = Declined ÷ screened. Manual-entry rate = quotes marked manual entry ÷ screened.

**Manager.** Team's day = due today, overdue, ASAP counts over open quotes. Pipeline by stage = count per status. Average cycle time = mean `total_seconds` from `v_quote_cycle_times`. Throughput this week = quotes reaching Approved/Sent to customer this week. Workload per quoter = `v_quoter_workload.open_quotes`. Decline rate = Declined ÷ decided. Revision rate = quotes entering In revision ÷ quoted. On-time rate = quotes completed by `needed_by` ÷ completed.

**Quoter.** Assigned to me = my open quotes. Due today/overdue/ASAP = my open quotes by `needed_by`/`priority`. Returned by reviser = my quotes in Returned to quoter. Completed this week = my quotes reaching Approved/Sent to customer this week. My average time per quote = mean `quoting_seconds` from `v_quote_cycle_times` over my quotes.

**Reviser.** Waiting for review = quotes in In revision or Returned to reviser. Oldest waiting = max age. Reviewed today = my transitions to Awaiting approval/Returned to quoter today. Edit rate = edited fields ÷ extracted fields from `v_ocr_accuracy`. Return rate = my returns ÷ my reviews.

**Approval (Manager dashboard).** Approval is a permission, not a role: Managers approve by default, and a Reviser can be given the Can approve permission. Awaiting approval = quotes in Awaiting approval. ASAP waiting = those with priority ASAP. Approved today = my transitions to Approved today. Average approval time = mean `approval_seconds` from `v_quote_cycle_times`. Quotes below margin floor = quotes with any line margin below the `minimum_margin` setting. The person who revised a quote cannot also approve it.

## Reporting Guidance

The views deliberately provide quote-level or user-level facts without prescribing a dashboard date range. In Power BI, use UTC timestamps and a shared date table; document whether a chart filters by quote creation date, status-change date, upload date, or final decision date. Those dates answer different questions. Do not infer stage time from the current status alone; use `v_quote_cycle_times` or the full status history.
